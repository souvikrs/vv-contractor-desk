# Contractor Desk (VV Automate) — demo

Front-end-only demo of an AI back office for building and road contractors (CPWD, state PWD).
Nine AI officers plus a Chief of Staff. Everything shown is sample data; no backend, no AI calls.

- Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
- Built 13 Nov 2026 for a first client conversation. Parked until the client gives his full requirements.
- Local only. Not pushed, not deployed.
